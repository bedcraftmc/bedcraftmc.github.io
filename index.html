Welcome to the Staff Team!

BedCraftMC Confidential Guide

Being a staff member is more than just having permissions and responsibilities—it's about helping create a positive, fair, and enjoyable environment for every player on the server. As a representative of the community, your actions, decisions, and attitude directly influence the player experience.

This guide has been created to help staff members understand their roles, responsibilities, and expectations. Whether you are a new recruit or an experienced moderator, these guidelines will provide a clear framework for handling situations professionally and consistently.

As a member of the staff team, you are expected to:

  • Treat all players with respect and professionalism.
  • Enforce server rules fairly and without bias.
  • Remain calm and objective during conflicts.
  • Assist players whenever possible.
  • Work together with fellow staff members as a team.
  • Set a positive example through your behavior both in-game and within the community.

Remember: Every action you take reflects on the entire staff team. Use your powers responsibly, communicate clearly, and always prioritize the health and growth of the community.

The Strike System

Accountability and disciplinary procedures

The Strike System exists to ensure that all staff members are held to the same standards of professionalism, activity, and conduct. A strike is not intended as a punishment alone—it serves as a warning and an opportunity for a staff member to correct their behavior.

First Strike — Verbal/Written Warning

Expires in 30 days

Issued for minor violations or first-time offenses. Consequences include a formal warning and discussion with management.

Examples:
  • Minor inactivity without notice.
  • Failure to follow a procedure.
  • Unprofessional behavior.
  • Incorrect use of commands.

Second Strike — Final Warning

Expires in 60 days

Issued when a staff member repeats offenses or commits a more serious violation. Consequences include increased monitoring or temporary suspension.

Examples:
  • Repeated inactivity.
  • Continued failure to follow procedures.
  • Multiple player complaints.
  • Abuse of authority (non-immediate).

Third Strike — Staff Removal

Indicates that sufficient opportunities for improvement have been provided. Results in immediate removal from the staff team.

Immediate Removal Offenses

Certain actions bypass the strike system entirely. Management reserves the right to remove a staff member immediately if their actions are deemed harmful.

  • Abuse of staff permissions for personal gain.
  • Cheating, hacking, or exploiting.
  • Sharing confidential staff information.
  • Harassment, discrimination, or severe toxicity.
  • Impersonating management or falsifying evidence.
  • Any action that significantly damages the server or community.

Appealing a Strike

Staff members have the right to appeal if they believe it was issued unfairly. You must remain professional, provide evidence, and accept the final decision. False or dishonest appeals may result in additional disciplinary action.

Promotions & Demotions

Evaluated at the end of every season

Staff ranks are earned through dedication and consistent effort, not solely through time spent on the team. Promotions and demotions help ensure that staff members are placed in roles that accurately reflect their performance and contributions.

Promotion Requirements

  • High levels of activity throughout the season.
  • Professional and respectful behavior.
  • Strong knowledge of server rules and procedures.
  • Positive interactions with players and staff.
  • Initiative in assisting the community.
  • Reliability when handling reports and tickets.
  • Leadership qualities and teamwork.

Demotion Criteria

  • Low activity throughout the season.
  • Poor performance in staff duties.
  • Repeated rule violations.
  • Lack of professionalism.
  • Failure to meet the expectations of current rank.
  • Inability to fulfill leadership responsibilities.

No Guaranteed Promotions

Meeting requirements does not guarantee a promotion. Management evaluates overall performance, activity records, conduct, and internal feedback. Promotions are never based solely on time spent on the team, friendships, previous ranks, or repeatedly asking for a promotion. Focus on performing your duties to the best of your ability.
